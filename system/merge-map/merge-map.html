What it adds up to

Stays the same

  • All the real work. Your context, decisions, brains, research, projects, audits and skills survive either way. Nothing gets lost.
  • His skills and files we already copied (/onboard, /audit, /level-up, connections.md) are in the same places already.
  • The router idea. One rulebook at the top that points to everything.

Would be different

  • Fewer top-level folders. system/, tools/, learning/ and exports/ fold into his references/, scripts/, projects/ and templates/.
  • One decisions log at decisions/log.md, his name for it.
  • One audit skill. Our os-audit checks would live inside /audit. Its "backtrack a miss" routine would need adding there.
  • Research as its own mini-OS, with a short research/CLAUDE.md of its own.

Not needed

  • Our blank template (templates/standard-ai-os-v1/). Started from Nate's kit, the kit is the blank copy.
  • What we'd gain instead: his /3d-brain skill, an archives/ habit (old files are moved there, never deleted) and his Codex sync script.

What it tells us

Same house, different room names. Building our own way didn't cost us anything real. The ideas match Nate's, because we learnt them from his videos. Mostly the names and folders differ.

For the set-up kit: this backs the recommendation to start beginners on Nate's kit. Everything we've added since then fits into his folders, so they'd grow into it without a redesign.

For this repo: no need to move anything now. Moving those files would break routes the audit checks, all for a tidier look. If you ever want it to match, the cheapest step is renaming tools/ to scripts/ (offered before), done in one go.
