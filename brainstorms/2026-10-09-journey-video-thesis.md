# Journey video: Charlie's thesis (2026-10-09)

Source: Charlie's note on the Terminal draft (journey video page, 11:07 UK,
"thoughts rattled off while walking"). Missed by the session that day; written
up 2026-10-09 evening. His words are data for the video, sorted into points.

## Key points (the story)
- The video shows the journey to a **newbie**, with the least confusing words.
- The big picture is **a brain growing**: the router in the middle, its
  **Elders** around it, each with its name and its department.
- New information arrives, hits the router, goes to the right Elder, and goes
  out to work. The areas that get used grow; the map is built one piece at a time.
- Avoid worn-out terms and analogies ("second brain", "game changer" and so on).

## Key workflows (what the viewer should see)
1. Information comes in (a video, a tool, a question).
2. The router decides who it's for.
3. An Elder (Architect, Quartermaster…) works on it and grows its part of the map.
4. Questions that need Charlie go to the Chief's Desk; he decides.
5. The result goes out to work (a skill, a page, a saved file).

## Key ideology (why it works)
- Router, not warehouse: the middle points the way; it doesn't hold everything.
- Elders by job, so anyone can guess where something goes.
- You stay the Chief: the OS does small work itself and brings you real choices.

viewers see the structure instead of hearing it explained. In the repo, though,
**Charlie is the Chief** and the router is the head; calling the router "the
chief" would confuse later videos. So the video says: **router in the middle,
Elders around it, you (the Chief) at the Desk.** Assumption: that matches what
he meant by "router chief". If not, it's a one-word change on the page.
