# Nate Herk on X: recurring themes

**Source:** `research/nate-herk/x-posts.md`: 366 posts from @nateherk, 24 May 2025 to 6 Oct 2026. Most are from April 2026 onwards. The 2025 posts are mainly n8n video plugs, and there is almost nothing between October 2025 and April 2026. 7 are reposts, which are left out below. Around 90 posts are just "Full video:" links, and about 30 are X Articles whose text was not captured, so many posts are only a headline. Built 2026-10-07. Everything here comes only from the posts. Like counts are as fetched.

## Themes in his own posts

**1. Head-to-head model tests on cost, speed and usage**
His most common original post. He runs the same prompt through two models, then posts the time, cost and how much of his plan it used. **His most frequent theme** (25+ posts, mostly Sept 2026).
- "GPT-6 Astra on Ultrafast ran for 19 minutes and ate 17% of my $500/mo plan" [2026-10-01](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2105676604517019870)
- "Across 8 builds, Codex spent ~$98 while Claude spent ~$444." [2026-08-26](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2092604154011136031)
- "Was disappointed with Grok 4.7 Disappointed with GPT-6 Sol Delighted with Opus 5.5" [2026-09-23](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2102597433125462461)

**2. Compare models at the same price, and match the model to the task**
He says to compare models priced alike, and pick by task: Sonnet when "done" is clear, Opus when you need a thinking partner. Held **consistently** (5 or 6 posts).
- "It makes more sense to compare products that are priced similarly." [2026-07-10](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2075602360303337669)
- "if you have a task with an objective definition of done, use Sonnet." [2026-09-28](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2104688136127471832)
- "Build your teams and workflows to be model-agnostic." [2026-09-16](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2100225765841174880)

**3. Trust how a model feels on your real work, not benchmarks**
Held **moderately** (2 or 3 posts), but argued at length.
- "Most people pick their AI model based on a benchmark. I pick mine based on feel." [2026-06-05](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2062891706886861180)
- "A big score is a marketing asset." [2026-06-05](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2062891706886861180)

**4. Free long courses, plugged on X**
He posts free YouTube courses of 1 to 10 hours (Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, Grok Bot, Claude Design, n8n). Held **constantly** (12+ launch posts, from 2025 to now).
- "I put a ton of work into this Claude Code course for normal people. It's completely free and 6 hours of content." [2026-07-11](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2075931356320690450)
- "I just dropped FREE 5 hour course on building and selling AI automations with Codex." [2026-09-21](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2102014103095034322)

**5. Hands-off agents: one /goal prompt, come back to a result**
He likes giving a long-running agent one goal and seeing what comes back, often a model directing cheaper worker agents. Held **strongly** (8+ posts, July to Oct 2026).
- "One /goal prompt to GPT 5.6 Sol and I got this. I didn't script, research, record, or edit. I did nothing." [2026-07-09](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2075338792785846404)
- "I gave Fable one /goal prompt, and came back to a business." [2026-07-08](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2074981997768802482)

**6. An "AI OS" built on wikis, memory and skills**
He runs his business from an AI operating system and gives away repos and skills (scroll-craft, token-dashboard, snagtime). Held **consistently** (8+ posts).
- "My AI OS runs my $5M/year business. It's all powered by LLM wikis." [2026-07-03](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2073165627582300276)
- "There are 4 major reasons AI messes up because of your context: → Poisoning → Bloat → Confusion → Clash" [2026-07-23](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2080424662803103874)

**7. Consulting: agree a metric first, use the simplest fix**
Drawn from client work. Agree the number before building, don't automate a process that doesn't exist yet, and only use AI when it is needed. Held **strongly** (5 long posts).
- "Now I don't start a project until we've agreed on the number we're trying to move and where it sits today." [2026-10-03](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2106414514501451979)
- "Automating a broken process just scales the problem." [2026-07-23](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2080324309441024441)
- "Start at the bottom. Only move up when the problem actually demands it." [2026-05-31](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2061081354914193656)

**8. You review it; your name is on it**
He uses AI to write, but you must review it and own the result. Held **consistently** (4 posts).
- "Our job changed from writer to reviewer. Most people haven't caught up." [2026-05-24](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2058649302248140878)
- "AI can move stupidly fast, but it needs trustworthy data, a clear definition of done, and someone willing to put their name on the final result." [2026-09-20](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2101773352750989526)

**9. Filter the hype: learn the "what", only learn the "how" when you need it**
Held **moderately** (2 or 3 posts). It is useful advice for beginners.
- "Staying aware does not mean testing everything." [2026-05-27](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2059723174607008250)
- "Does this solve a problem I have today? If not, it's good to know about. Not good to go learn right now." [2026-05-27](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2059723174607008250)

**10. "We live in a little AI bubble"**
He keeps pointing out that most people barely use AI. Held **moderately** (3 posts).
- "Only 2.2% of US households are paying for AI... we are very early and we do live in a little AI bubble." [2026-10-03](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2106397544511140163)
- "The average person opens up ChatGPT a few times a month for a 'Google search'" [2026-09-18](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2100984279463285067)

**11. Safety and incentives (measured, not doom)**
He is pro-AI but wants safety to pay commercially. Held **lightly** (3 or 4 posts, Sept 2026).
- "I think the hardest problem in AI safety is changing what gets rewarded." [2026-09-16](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2100275411393359899)
- "'incentive exists' doesn't mean 'crisis is fake'." [2026-09-18](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2101014015967113627)

**12. Anger at people reposting his videos without credit**
Held **strongly** but rarely (2 or 3 posts).
- "He printed half a million views in 24 hours by posting my full course without at least a tag" [2026-09-20](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2101656269056610682)

**Nuance and humour to recognise:** "AI has made me 10x more productive but 10x lazier at the same time" ([2026-09-17](https://x.com/nateherk/status/2100679077900308984)). Jokes include "My last YouTube video got 1M views if you add 950k" and "If they weren't there at localhost:3000, don't let them in at .com". "I solved prompt engineering" is almost certainly a joke, but its media wasn't captured.
