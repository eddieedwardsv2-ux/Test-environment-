# Nick Saraev on X: recurring themes

**Source:** `research/nick-saraev/x-posts.md`: 359 posts from @nicksaraev, 14 Apr to 5 Oct 2026. Nearly all are from 1 Jul onwards, plus 9 posts from mid-April. 8 are reposts, which are left out below. Built 2026-10-07. Everything here comes only from the posts. Like counts are as fetched.

## Themes in his own posts

**1. AI progress is unstoppable, so don't bet against it**
He thinks we are in or near "the Singularity" and that society will be reshaped whatever happens next. He sees the stakes as close to all or nothing. Held **strongly and often** (10+ posts).
- "All model progress could stop today and we'd still be reorganizing our societies around it one hundred years from now." [2026-10-03](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2106205001685315664)
- "AI gets misaligned, we lose control... -> we all die or are hopelessly disempowered. Tough." [2026-09-09](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2097711055455900020)

**2. AI music and art are here to stay, and the backlash will pass**
He compares the AI music backlash to past fights over microphones, synths and sampling. He argues AI makes everyone an artist. Held **very strongly** in a burst of about 8 posts in late September, and it is his most-liked material.
- "Spotify, Apple Music, and streaming in general is over." [2026-09-28](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2104612590554861742)
- "Yes, this probably hurts your feelings. It's still going to happen." [2026-09-29](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2104779570641780881)

**3. Run agents like staff: you scope the tasks, they do the work, you check it**
He treats AI as a team you manage. Tasks go into Linear, are scoped well, run overnight or on loops, and come back for QA. He says this is for business work, not just coding. Held **consistently** (about 8 posts, mostly in July).
- "The way to use AI most efficiently, imo, is as a project manager... tag them with the specific AI you want to complete it" [2026-07-02](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2072750734966473049)
- "a big chunk of future productivity will be articulating goals into agent-ready prompts and sending one in before you go to bed" [2026-07-25](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2081127913949376783)
- "You only need 2 examples to pass something off to an agent... Not all things need workflows" [2026-07-01](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2072359197933732058)

**4. Human work shifts to checking quality and taste**
He believes knowledge workers will follow coders, moving from producing work to verifying it. He thinks human traits will keep their value even when AI is better at the task. Held **moderately** (4 or 5 posts).
- "coders have almost entirely shifted from writing code -> verifying it. You will do that too, with deliverables!" [2026-07-02](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2072500350075142211)
- "the bottleneck is not production, it is evaluating the work of AI production" [2026-07-12](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2076338089195438174)

**5. Don't let AI write in your voice ("AI smell")**
He is pro-AI but hostile to AI-written messages, press releases and comments. He says he now avoids em dashes and "it's not X, it's Y" phrasing because "humanness is the signal". Held **strongly** (6+ posts).
- "you can not be outsourcing your tone of voice to these things! It is SO obvious and immediately makes people lose respect for you" [2026-07-27](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2081730499979198489)
- "PR is not the place to automate!" [2026-07-23](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2080276283733463307)
- "if I spot an AI one I right click and ban" [2026-07-12](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2076325419914306002)

**6. Volume, speed and showing up daily**
He puts his results down to doing far more than others and doing it faster. That means daily output, timed tasks and "1,000 reps". This is **his most constant theme** (dozens of posts, many of them daily logs).
- "I publish ~100 mins a day. Avg YouTuber does maybe ~30 mins a week" [2026-08-02](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2083964166793244963)
- "Showing up is the foundation. Volume is next. Iterating is after that." [2026-09-26](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2103656957601333694)

**7. Deal with the market often and think in expected value**
His idea of success is to "take lots of swings", put what you already have to good use, and rank your options by rough expected value. He also says success comes from working with people. Held **consistently** (5 or 6 posts).
- "the straightest line path is to interact with the market as often and for as long as you can... the market is a big sieve" [2026-04-15](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2044471925775507690)
- "Tabulate -> allocate -> iterate!" [2026-07-05](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2073765228626784305)
- "most success in my life has come from working with other people" [2026-09-16](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2100282328568492073)

**8. Paid communities are the best business model (Maker School and Skool)**
He calls communities "the end-game evolution of information products". He runs Maker School as the paid tier and Maker Zero as a free funnel into it. He posts revenue, member counts and conversion rates in public. **This is his single most frequent topic** (40+ posts).
- "Communities are the end-game evolution of information products." [2026-04-16](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2044827515974492575)
- "I have 111 Skool Posts to respond to today... this makes me $275,777 per month" [2026-08-26](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2092606093939646570)
- "Maker Zero -> Maker School conversion rate stats are in: 0.55%" [2026-07-17](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2078199986223972783)

**9. Raw, unedited content, repurposed everywhere**
He prefers one-take, lightly edited videos and long courses. His plan is to cut each recording into clips for many accounts using AI plus one helper, not a media team. Held **strongly** (10+ posts).
- "Record in one shot, publish immediately with no/few edits - Dont look at metrics for at least 30d" [2026-07-02](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2072826030226862181)
- "Longform courses really are the key for me" [2026-08-23](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2091574858694131826)

**10. Use AI to fix your own life problems now**
He pushes people to describe their problems out loud and let AI solve them. His example: models analysing his sleep data. Held **consistently** (6+ posts).
- "I am literally always five minutes away from solving problems I've procrastinated for months" [2026-07-11](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2075737335187263844)
- "walk around your home > name whatever inconveniences you. Then feed that list into an AI" [2026-07-21](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2079383932223762735)

**11. Caveat: old-fashioned focus still matters**
This pulls against themes 3 and 10. He says AI may be hurting attention spans, that his money still comes from writing and calls, and that writing by hand feels good. Held **lightly** (3 or 4 posts), but it is a real nuance.
- "most of my money is still made from extremely 'old fashioned' focus" [2026-07-02](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2072505593244570112)
- "attention spans and focus have fallen off a cliff... AI has great promise for the collective, yet impacts you greatly on an individual level" [2026-08-30](https://x.com/nicksaraev/status/2094049207120429475)

**Humour to recognise:** "Fable 6 can't even solve world hunger. Useless." is *sarcasm*, mocking people who complain about models. Also joking: "saturate Earth with my face and bake myself into the neurons of AGI", "Eventually, President" (the local-politics ads post), and "Must be all the wildfire smoke".
