## What they value when judging a tool
- **Saves a real chore.** Corey on email: "search sucks." (c5Z 8:08). He wants it found by text, fast: "it found it within like 60 seconds." (c5Z 8:08)
- **Simple and cheap.** "Love onetime fees." (c5Z 10:10) and "it's pay per use, too, which again I love." (c5Z 16:17)
- **Seeing the output first.** "you can see exactly what the output's going to look like before you buy it." (c5Z 14:16)
- **Clean, not "vibe coded".** "it doesn't have that vibecoded feel either." (c5Z 17:17)
- **Pieces joined up, not invented.** "he didn't invent any of it." (c5Z 15:46)

## Red flags they mention
- **Nagging.** "It's just doing nothing but nagging me about stuff" (c5Z 3:33)
- **Not different enough.** "I don't see it being that unique enough for me to go and test it out" (c5Z 5:34)
- **Agent sprawl.** "agents now every single day." (c5Z 5:03)
- **Ethics, lightly.** "I don't know that I would mess with this" (c5Z 2:02) about the fake influencer; Corey brushes off "the people that try to hold out" (c5Z 2:32).
- **Slowing the machine.** "my computer's gotten sluggish and more and more sluggish" (c5Z 9:39)

## How they decide they'd use it
- **Personal use, said plainly.** The host: "This one is the app that I'm going to rebuild my whole MacBook around." (c5Z 8:38)
- **Sign up straight away.** "this is something that I signed up for" (c5Z 12:12), and "Congratulations on your first customers." (c5Z 19:50)
- **Low risk to try.** "Like, why not go and apply?" (c5Z 7:07)

## Where we differ
Of 12 tools: 5 agree, 5 disagree, 2 n/a (`our_view` in `weeks/2026-W41.json`).
- **Their lens is "can I earn from it". Ours is "do I have the pain".** Rule 39 (adopt tools only for real pain) turns most of their no-brainers into "no" for Charlie: Boring Funnels, TinyPopups, VMPal.
- **Access.** Corey gives Instinct his email and card. Rule 36 says read-only first, and this repo is public, so we say no.
- **Fake influencer.** The host hesitates; Corey says you'll be left behind. We side with the host (Rule 37, check before anything goes public).
- **Testing.** They sign up on first sight. Rule 43 says measure on our own work first (CreativeClaw, Dots).
- **Where we agree.** Hark is not worth testing, and applying for Claude for Startups costs nothing (after checking UK eligibility).
