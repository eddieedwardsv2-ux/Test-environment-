- **Speed.** "Also, it was faster than everybody else." (6Ix 7:49)

## Red flags
- **Untrusted installs.** "if you don't trust where you installed them, things can get pretty bad pretty quick." (1fH 16:23)

- **Nothing to stand on.** "There's not much for them to stand on" (6xi 14:25)

## Where we differ
Of 29 tools: stance 11 would use, 13 mixed, 1 wouldn't use, 4 no opinion; we agree on 21, disagree on 4, 4 n/a. Need: 1 yes, 8 maybe, 20 no.
- **Their pain is not Charlie's.** Travel searches, WordPress, team boards and clothes drive their excitement. Rule 39: adopt a tool only for real pain (Muse, GrokBot, Worktrunk, Aesty disagree).
- **Parallel agents.** Adam says worktrees "going to change your life" (1fH 17:24); Charlie runs one session. Rule 39 caps parallel sessions.
- **Big skill packs.** ECC and Agent Skills get 261,000 and 95,000 stars; Rule 34 says skills do one job, so borrow the habit, not the pack.
- **Trust.** They sign up fast; Rules 36 and 37 (read-only first, vet plugins, public repo) say wait.
- **Versus the pilot.** Less money talk, more which agent to trust, and a split in the hosts' own views: 13 of 29 are mixed (pilot: sign-up-first). hyperframes is the one clear "yes" for Charlie.
