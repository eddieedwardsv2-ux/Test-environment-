## Where we differ
Of 12 tools: 5 would use, 6 mixed, 1 no opinion; we agree on 6, disagree on 5, 1 n/a.
- **Hands-off vs drafts first.** Affan's agent drafts and sends DMs from his accounts with 1Password credentials. Rule 36 says drafts first with a human approving; Rules 33 and 37 add a separate checker and a check before anything goes public.
- **Their pain is not Charlie's.** Credits, caps and subscription bills drive their excitement. Rule 39: adopt a tool only for real pain; Charlie has no credit bill.
- **Overlap.** Dyad, Vane, Odysseus and OpenManus duplicate Claude Code or Codex (Rule 42).
- **Test first.** The host's own tests of ACE-Step and OpenShorts were weak; try one real video on the hosted version before installing anything (Rule 43).
- **Versus the pilot.** The pilot hosts frame tools as money-makers; this week is cost-cutting, privacy and replacing subscriptions. The scepticism ("not as good as", "days are numbered") is stronger, with fewer "no-brainer" calls.
