## What they value when judging a tool
- **No per-seat or usage fees.** "you don't have to pay per seat" (kMP 25:22); "nine repos that will make you money and cost nothing" (kMP 0:01).
- **A real company behind it.** "this is actually brought to you like by a real business." (CQh 4:34)
- **A human choosing the style.** "I love galleries like this" (CQh 15:46).
- **Acting at once, not on a timer.** "you want that immediiacy." (o7R 3:34)
- **Agents that don't keep checking in.** "you're no longer my employee. Now, you're my boss." (dfF 24:01)

## Red flags they mention
- **Token bills.** "My $140 a month plan suddenly had overages of $265 for just two days of extra usage." (o7R 9:11)
- **Constant confirmations.** "notice how he still has to confirm every freaking message?" (dfF 11:13)
- **AI-made fluff.** "Docs written by AI I are hyper verbose." (CQh 9:38)
- **Untrusted plugins.** "please be careful at what's running on your computer." (CQh 20:52)
- **Personal data in a shared space.** "I'm concerned about integrating it in with the whole team." (bTb 14:50)
- **Approvals pile up in one thread.** "approve one or three or five or 10 uh refunds in the same thread." (o7R 24:37)

## How they decide they'd use it
- **Try it, then fold it in.** "I'm going to start to integrate this into my system." (o7R 33:19); "So, I use this after watching him." (o7R 15:19)
- **Free first, pay if hosting hurts.** "it goes from free if you host it yourself, 10 bucks a month if you let them deal with the hosting" (kMP 4:32)
- **Another worker for the main agent.** "now Meta is another agent for it to send work to." (dfF 25:32)
- **Study by using.** "I want to study all these things by personally using it and watching the best videos." (dfF 26:03)

## Where we differ (this week)
Of 24 tools: 10 agree, 9 disagree, 5 n/a (`our_view` in `weeks/2026-W40.json`). Stances: 12 would use, 8 mixed, 1 wouldn't, 3 no opinion.
- **Check-ins.** The host wants agents that never ask; we keep the ask (Rule 36, drafts first). Biggest clash (Meta Muse).
- **"Best" without our pain.** GrokBot is "the best", yet he paid $265 extra in two days. We say no: Charlie has Claude Code and Codex (Rules 39, 35).
- **Business tools.** PostHog, Invoice Ninja, Cal.diy, Autumn, Frappe CRM, Chatwoot: all "would use" for him, all "no" for us (no customers, Rule 39).
- **Vs the pilot.** Still money-first, but more critical of vendors and of bills; fewer instant sign-ups. Same pattern of liking "free" and distrusting lock-in.
- **Where we agree.** Plugins need vetting (Rule 37); skill piles are bloat (Rule 34); Impeccable is worth a trial.
