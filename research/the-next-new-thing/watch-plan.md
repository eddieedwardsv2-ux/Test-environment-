## Skip these 5 types

1. **Money-promise titles**, e.g. "9 shocking repos that make money", "He earns
   $5k building agents", "$100K/Year AI Business". They sell an outcome, not a
   skill, and they're the hype you said you don't want.
2. **Founder and investor interviews (45–60 min)**, e.g. Polsia, Elad Gil, Garry
   Tan, Morning Brew. They're about running a company. Too long and too early
   for you.
3. **Sales, cold-call and lead-gen automation**, e.g. "AI sales emails", "cold
   calls", "100 Clients a Week". These are for a business you haven't started yet.
   Park them with the decorating business.
4. **Model-news hot takes**, e.g. "Opus 4.7 is here", "LEAK: Anthropic will copy
   Lovable", "Claude's Mythos too powerful?". They go out of date within weeks
   and won't teach you a method.
5. **OpenClaw and Paperclip agent-farm setup**, e.g. "run multiple Clawdbots",
   "Do NOT use Claude in OpenClaw". These are third-party agent stacks on top of
   tools you're still learning. Learn Claude Code and Codex first.

Also go carefully with the weekly "Top 10 repos" round-ups (30–40 min). Only
skim them for one named tool you actually need.
