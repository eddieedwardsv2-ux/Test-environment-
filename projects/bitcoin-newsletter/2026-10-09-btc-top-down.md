# BTC: A Good Long Into Bad Resistance

**Market structure newsletter · Issue 001 · 9 October 2026**  
**Pair:** BTC/USDT · **Timeframes:** Monthly / 12H / 1H  
**Status:** Draft for editorial review · Chart-based analysis, not a verified live feed

## The thesis in 30 seconds

Bitcoin has reacted from the $80K–$81K higher-timeframe demand area and reclaimed the $82.2K–$82.6K support band. That's enough to support a tactical long thesis. It is **not** enough to declare a higher-timeframe trend reversal.

The trade is straightforward: defend reclaimed support, work through $83.6K, then challenge $86.8K. The bigger reversal only becomes credible if BTC breaks and holds $86.8K. After that, $87K–$88K is a liquidity pocket and $93K–$96K is the serious monthly supply obstacle.

A decent long and a weak macro chart can coexist. No need to make them agree.

## 1 / Monthly: buyers have work to do

The monthly rebound from the region around $58K–$60K is substantial. So is the overhead supply. Price is now negotiating an old monthly decision area around $82.5K–$83K, after trading much lower earlier in the year.

There are three distinct questions here.

First: can buyers secure $82.5K–$83K as support on a meaningful closing basis? Second: can BTC reclaim the repeatedly defended $86.8K weekly barrier? Third: should that happen, what response comes at $93K–$96K, the large monthly supply zone?

Skipping from the bounce to a six-figure target ignores two resistance shelves between here and there.

The monthly downside references are $76.3K, then around $67K. Neither is a prediction; they're the next mapped areas to reassess if this recovery fails.

**Monthly read:** recovery underway; major reversal unconfirmed. The current monthly candle is unfinished.

## 2 / 12H: three rejections are evidence, not a countdown

The chart shows roughly three prominent failures around **$86.8K**. The temptation is to say: first test strongest, fourth test breaks.

That's too neat.

Repeated tests can absorb resting supply, yes. But a resistance shelf is not automatically weaker after each touch. What matters is the *quality* of the response: are buyers printing higher lows, making shallower pullbacks and spending more time near the ceiling? Or is each rejection delivering meaningful downside displacement?

The latest swing back towards $82.2K does not scream exhausted sellers. The burden of proof is still on the bulls.

A confirmed break and hold above $86.8K would change the structure. Until then, resistance is still resistance.

**12H read:** support-to-resistance range, not a confirmed breakout.

## 3 / 1H: a trade, not a religion

The lower-timeframe bullish case is more compelling:

1. BTC swept lower into the H12 / weekly bullish order-block region near $80K–$81K.
2. The response was sharp enough to reclaim the ~$82.2K weekly support.
3. Price moved back above the previous week's low, around $82.6K.
4. The H1 bullish breaker / order-block area around $82.6K–$82.8K became the near-term retest zone.

That's a coherent trading framework. There is a location for entry analysis, somewhere to be wrong, and defined upside obstacles. Not a promise of a bottom.

For the tactical thesis, **holding $82.2K–$82.6K matters more than guessing the next candle**. Acceptance below that zone undermines the reclaim and calls for reassessment. Position-specific stops depend on entry and execution, and are not implied by this newsletter.

First obstacle: around $83.6K. Next: $86.8K.

## Scenario planning, not target worship

**Base case — tactical bounce:** $82.2K–$82.6K holds; BTC attempts $83.6K and potentially $86.8K. This is the near-term long argument.

**Bull expansion:** price accepts above $86.8K and defends a retest. That brings $87K–$88K liquidity into play first, followed by the more consequential $93K–$96K supply shelf. $102.4K is an extended reference, not an immediate target.

**Bear failure:** BTC fails to defend the reclaimed weekly zone and starts closing back below it. The bounce thesis loses its footing; $80K–$81K, then the monthly $76.3K reference, become increasingly relevant.

## Bottom line

**Monthly:** the rally still has overhead supply.  
**12H:** $86.8K has rejected BTC repeatedly.  
**1H:** there is a defensible tactical long while support holds.

The mistake is forcing those three observations into one all-or-nothing bias.

Buyers don't get credit for a breakout before price breaks out. Sellers don't get credit for a breakdown while support is being defended.

Trade what's in front of you. Change your mind when the chart changes.
