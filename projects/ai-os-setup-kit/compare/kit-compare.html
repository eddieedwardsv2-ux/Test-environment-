Kit Compare

A blank template is an AI OS with all the folders, rules and skills in place but nothing personal in it yet. It's like a new house: the rooms are built and labelled, but the furniture (who you are, your goals, your projects) arrives when you move in. Each person gets their own private copy, and an interview fills it in.

We have two of these. Ours (templates/standard-ai-os-v1/) is the small one we built from Nate's videos. Nate's AIS-OS kit is his free official starter kit, which we adopted parts of. Tap any file to see what it's for. In ours, you can read the actual text.

Recommendation: Nate's kit, plus three things from ours

Nate's kit does the hard part for a beginner. One /onboard interview personalises everything, and it comes with the scored audit and the weekly level-up. Ours is smaller and easier to understand, and it does three things better than his. Add these when setting someone up:

  1. One rulebook: make CLAUDE.md just the line @AGENTS.md, so the two copies can't drift apart.
  2. The fresh-session test: open a new session and ask "who am I, what's my priority, where do things go?" This is already step 6 of the plan.
  3. The research guide (research/README.md), for later, when they have 30+ notes.
