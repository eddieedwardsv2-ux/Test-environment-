## Audience research
### Decorators' Facebook group thread (2026-10-07, ~30 of 60 comments seen)
What people *said* (anonymised — group posts aren't ours to republish):
- Most-liked worry: office workers losing jobs to AI will move into
  decorating, so more competition and fewer customers who can pay.
- Common jokes/dismissal: "walls will be screens", robots, "Skynet".
- One decorator uses AI for quote wording (helps with dyslexia) and for
  showing customers what a finished job could look like.
- Someone asked him "out of curiosity, what do you use it for?" — curiosity exists.
- Nobody named a specific AI tool or feature.

Theories (not yet evidence): few tradespeople use AI; those who do get poor
results from weak prompts; some pay for trade software with dated AI features.
