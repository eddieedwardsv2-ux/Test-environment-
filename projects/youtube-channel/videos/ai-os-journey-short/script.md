# Final spoken script

1. I started with a phone and a lot of questions. Could I build my own AI operating system without coding?
2. First, I learned the parts: a model to think, tools to act, skills to guide it, and saved context.
3. Then I built a Router. New information goes to the right specialist. I call these specialists my Elders.
4. The Router points the way. It loads the useful context, instead of dumping every note into every answer.
5. I stay the Chief. Small, reversible jobs can move ahead. Real choices come to my Desk, where I decide.
6. The Quartermaster helps pick the tools. The Architect helps organise the system. Their work becomes something I can use.
7. But a confident answer isn't proof. We plant faults and check the result. I'm still proving the foundations work.
8. Next, help a friend build their own system, and document it. One useful step at a time.
